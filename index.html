Vegetables

Carrots are crunchy, sweet root vegetables. They are rich in beta-carotene and vitamin A, which are great for maintaining healthy vision.

Corn is a sweet, versatile cereal grain with golden kernels. It is a great source of energy and fiber, enjoyed worldwide on the cob or as a staple ingredient.

Bell peppers are crisp, refreshing vegetables with a mild flavor. They are packed with vitamins C and antioxidants, adding vibrant color and crunch to any dish.
